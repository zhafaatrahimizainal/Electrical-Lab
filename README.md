## 📌 Features

- **⚡ Interactive Circuit Simulation**: Build, test, and analyze AC/DC electrical circuits in real-time right from your browser.
- **🔌 Component Library**:
  - Resistors, Capacitors, Inductors
  - DC Power Sources & AC Signal Generators
  - Switches, Relays, Diodes, LEDs, and Transistors
- **📊 Measuring Instruments**:
  - Digital Multimeters (Voltmeter, Ammeter, Ohmmeter)
  - Virtual Oscilloscopes for waveform and signal analysis
- **📚 Lab Manuals & Experiments**:
  - **Ohm's Law & Kirchhoff's Laws** (KCL & KVL)
  - **Network Theorems**: Thevenin, Norton, Superposition, & Maximum Power Transfer
  - **AC Circuits**: Series/Parallel RLC Resonance & Impedance analysis
  - **Filters**: Low-pass, High-pass, and Band-pass active/passive filters
- **📱 Responsive & Fast**: Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers with light/dark theme support.
